The problem
02 / 15

CSR tree-planting today is a number you can't trust

What corporates get now

  • A logo on a map — 10,000 trees collapse into one pin.
  • A certificate and a "trees planted" count. No survival, no growth, no proof.
  • Carbon/oxygen numbers that are linear and inflated — indefensible to an auditor.
  • A static one-photo tree page. No way to verify the tree is even alive.

What the law now demands

  • Section 135 + Schedule VII(iv): spend only counts if planting + survival are proven.
  • Rule 8(3): independent impact assessment on every project ≥ ₹1 cr.
  • BRSR Core: board-signed, externally assured ESG numbers — top 500 (FY25-26) → top 1,000 (FY26-27).
  • Green Credits: only become a tradable asset once survival is verified.
⚠️ The gap: "trees planted" is the #1 reporting failure. Nobody shows the living tree over time with audit-grade proof. That gap is the product.

System deep-dive
05 / 15

Every module, already built

Public & sponsor

Clustered satellite map (per-tree health pins + boundary) · per-tree proof page (growth chart, visit timeline, photos, QR, verification badges) · sponsor portal with CSV export · public carbon-methodology page · public "verify any tree".

Field capture

Offline-first PWA: planter logs in, captures GPS + photo + status + height + DBH, queues to device, syncs on reconnect. Planter role is capture-only — locked out of every admin surface.

Carbon & O₂ engine

Chave-2014: AGB = 0.0673·(WD·DBH²·H)^0.976 · per-species wood density (ICRAF/GWD) · −18% permanence buffer · −10% uncertainty · O₂ tied to CO₂ (0.727). Monitored vs planted-baseline separated.

360° tours

Photo Sphere Viewer walk-through per plot · tree hotspots (yaw/pitch) → open tree proof · Street-View scene links between capture points · graceful flat-image fallback.

Reporting & certificates

Branded print-to-PDF sponsor / forest / tree reports · per-tree certificate + QR · gift recipient (name, email, message) · single + bulk certificate email via Resend.

Integrity

SHA-256 photo-hash dedup (recycled-photo fraud) · geo-plausibility flags · demo data flagged + excluded · carbon ledger Merkle-rooted + timestamped to Bitcoin (OpenTimestamps).

Stack: React + Vite + TS · Express + TS · PostgreSQL (Neon) · Vercel · Leaflet + MarkerCluster · Photo Sphere Viewer · Resend · OpenTimestamps. Roles: SuperAdmin / Admin / Planter.

What the CSR / ESG buyer gets

  • One audit-ready dataset that satisfies all three: the Rule 8(3) impact assessor, the BRSR Core assurer, and the Green Credit registry.
  • Per-tree geotag + survival at 1/3/5 yrs + species + timestamped photos = defensible MRV trail.
  • Converts soft CSR cash into compliant, bookable 2% spend that survives an impact assessment.
  • De-risks the board-signed BRSR filing — removes a greenwashing / restatement liability.
  • Opens Green Credits (tradable only once survival is proven).

The obligations driving demand

  • Section 135 — 2% CSR mandate (net worth ≥₹500 cr / turnover ≥₹1,000 cr / PAT ≥₹5 cr). Statutory, not optional.
  • Schedule VII(iv) — the legal hook for trees; counts only if planting + survival proven.
  • Rule 8(3) — independent impact assessment on every project ≥₹1 cr.
  • BRSR Core — reasonable assurance, ~49 KPIs, phasing to top 1,000 by FY26-27.
  • Survival norms — physical verification at 12–18 months; 40% canopy at 5 yrs.
💡 We don't sell trees — we sell the proof that makes the spend legal and the brand safe. That's why the buyer is the price-insensitive compliance budget, not a charity donor.

Phase 3 · dMRV vertical

Buys the valuation. Sharpen into carbon/agroforestry dMRV with outcome pricing once you hold audited projects. Highest ceiling + moat.

→ per-ha / per-tonne / %-of-savings

First-client motion (no track record)

  • Offer one funder a fixed 1,000–5,000-tree site at near-cost — the artifact becomes your portfolio.
  • Subcontract planting/care to an existing NGO; never build field ops from zero.
  • Deliver under the NGO's / corporate's brand (white-label) to borrow their trust.
  • Convert the delivered site into a reference → raise price on deal two.
  • For the vertical leap, co-run one paid POC on a partner's existing carbon project — their name is your trust proxy.

  • Digital MRV is a clear cost-pain wedge (manual ₹380/ha/yr → digital ₹52). You don't have to invent demand.
