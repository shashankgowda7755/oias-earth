1. Executive summary

Most tree-planting platforms sell a birth certificate: a geotag and a photo at planting day. After that, nobody knows if the tree lived. We sell a life record: a longitudinal, independently verifiable trail proving the tree is still alive — every monitoring visit dated, measured, photographed, and tamper-evident — and we package that proof into the carbon, oxygen, and survival reports that corporate CSR buyers need to justify their spend.

2. The problem & the insight

Corporates spend on tree-planting CSR and get a spreadsheet + some planting-day photos. They cannot prove to an auditor, a journalist, or their own board that the trees survived. Survival in real plantings is 60–90%, not 100% — yet every competitor reports "100% alive" because they never check. The day someone measures one tree, the claim collapses.

The insight: the scarce, defensible thing is not the tree — it's the continuously verified proof the tree is alive. That proof is what makes a CSR report audit-grade, what justifies a premium price, and what nobody else is doing. Build the proof engine; everything else (gifting, CSR, carbon) sells off the same verified dataset.

3. The product (what it does)

Three layers on one verified dataset:

Layer 1 — Capture

  • Field PWA (/field): planters log in (Planter role), pick their assigned forest, capture each visit — GPS + in-app photo + status/height/diameter/date. Offline-first, syncs on reconnect.
  • Box-grid coordinate engine: for dense plantings (e.g. 1-ft spacing), per-tree GPS is useless (consumer GPS resolves ~3–5 m). Instead: capture the 4 corners of a planting box + the grid (rows×cols, spacing) → we compute every tree's exact position to the centimetre. 5,000 trees = 50 corner-readings, not 5,000 GPS fixes.

Layer 2 — Verify

  • Carbon ledger (append-only) — per-visit CO₂e from measured growth (see §4).
  • Oxygen generated, derived from the same measured carbon.
  • Integrity checks: every photo SHA-256-hashed (catches recycled-photo fraud); GPS >5 km from the forest centre flagged as suspect.
  • Tamper-evidence: carbon ledger Merkle-rooted and anchored to Bitcoin via OpenTimestamps (free, no wallet). anchor job pending

Layer 3 — Experience & Reporting

  • Public heartbeat map — satellite, forests pinned, drill into a forest to see each tree coloured by health; boundary polygon; NDVI cross-check.
  • Per-tree proof page — life record: visit timeline, growth chart, survival verdict, carbon + oxygen, QR plaque.
  • Interactive 360° tour — walk the site Street-View-style; every sapling is a clickable hotspot → its record; auto-placed from the grid coordinates.
  • Sponsor portal — a corporate's forests, live stats, searchable tree register.
  • Reports (the money): downloadable, branded CSR impact report (carbon + oxygen + survival + per-forest + map + methodology) and a per-tree gift certificate.

6. Unit economics (carbon side, indicative)

  • 1 surviving native tree ≈ 0.4–0.9 tCO₂e over 20 years.
  • Carbon gross ≈ ₹9–30 / mature tree / year; ~zero in years 1–3; first issuance 2–3 years out.
  • A 10,000-tree forest ≈ $1–3k / year carbon — modest. Carbon is the credibility lever, not the cash cow.
  • Rigorous dMRV lifts a project's carbon rating band: roughly B-grade (~$11/t) → A-grade (~$40/t). That re-rating, not volume, is the carbon upside.
  • B2C gifting + B2B CSR are the near-term cash; carbon compounds later.

7. Moat & differentiation

  • Proof, not promise. We show the living tree + its measured trail; competitors promise and never show.
  • Integrity as brand. We publish dead trees. The first faked record would kill the moat — so we never fake one.
  • Honest carbon. Scientific allometry + buffer + uncertainty, labelled estimated. Smaller, true numbers survive audit; big fragile ones don't.
  • Grid-derived geo + auto-hotspots. Centimetre placement of thousands of trees from a few corner readings — and the 360 hotspots place themselves from that geometry.

10. Risks & open items

  • No real data yet. The whole thing is demo/imported. The business is real only once a real forest is captured. Highest-priority de-risk.
  • DBH convention. Diameter must be measured at breast height (1.3 m) or carbon is wrong + a verifier rejects it. Needs a field SOP.
  • Storage. Photos/360 uploads need the Blob token enabled or they don't persist.
  • Carbon over-promise. Never call estimated numbers "credits". Don't lead the pitch with carbon revenue.
  • Legal/IP. Clean-room must be maintained (no forking the licensed incumbent); platform IP vs tenant data must be papered before any resale.

11. Integrity rules (non-negotiable)

  • Never fake a record. Demo/simulated data is always labelled "demonstration / not a verified capture".
  • Publish dead trees. Honest survival is the brand.
  • Clean-room. Original build; do not fork the incumbent or reuse its keys/assets.
  • Vocabulary. "Permanent verified record / living proof / tamper-proof timeline." Avoid "NFT"/"crypto" in consumer/CSR copy. No tradable tokens.
  • Carbon labelling. "Estimated / verification-ready removal" until a registry verifies — never "credit".

12. Legal / registry path

  • Entity: Indian Section-8 SPV; treat carbon/data revenue as export-of-services (not FCRA); GST registered. to set up
  • Registry: launch on Plan Vivo (low barrier, smallholder/NGO fit, ex-ante cash), build the data product to Verra VM0047 census spec, dual-list later. Avoid India CCTS for foreign sales (domestic price low, exclusivity).
  • Only post-1-Jan-2025 plantings are creditable; baseline / additionality / permanence package + land-tenure / carbon-rights needed before any sale.
