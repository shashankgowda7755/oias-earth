Why these prices hold (market context)

  • Demand engine: India CSR = ₹34,909 cr FY24; environment/afforestation slice ~₹3,000–3,500 cr/yr — recurring, compliance-driven, price-insensitive.
  • MID anchor (verified): Earth5R Maharashtra ₹80 L project, monitoring ₹8 L = ~10% of budget → the norm MID is built on.
  • HIGH statutory lever (verified, corrected): Rule 8(3) caps bookable impact-assessment expense at the lesser of 5% of CSR spend or ₹50 L. HIGH at ₹49.5 L stays fully CSR-bookable; mandatory only for ₹10 cr+ obligation buyers on projects ≥ ₹1 cr.
  • dMRV is the cheap layer (verified): Anaxee digital MRV ₹52/ha/yr vs ₹380 manual (86% cut) — survival/verification is what we sell, not planting.
  • Credibility argument: CAMPA mandates 60–65% survival but enforcement is weak (Uttarakhand 33.5%, Odisha ~7.5% audited) — that gap is exactly why corporates pay for provable monitoring.
